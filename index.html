Have more questions? Our specialists are here to help you choose the right treatment for your skin and hair concerns.

How much does a consultation with a dermatologist at Vtiara Clinic cost? ⌄
Consultation fees vary by treatment type and doctor availability. Please call us or book online for the most current pricing. We also offer special packages for combined treatments.
What types of skincare products do dermatologists at Vtiara Clinic recommend? ⌄
Our dermatologists recommend clinically proven skincare products suited to your skin type and condition. After your consultation, you'll receive a personalized skincare routine.
Where is Vtiara Clinic located? ⌄
We have branches in Indiranagar, Bangalore and New BEL Road, Bangalore. Please contact us for exact directions and branch timings.
How is a dermatologist different from a skin-care specialist? ⌄
A dermatologist is a licensed medical doctor who specializes in diagnosing and treating skin, hair, and nail conditions. They can prescribe medications and perform clinical procedures, unlike general skincare specialists.
How can I schedule an appointment with a dermatologist at Vtiara Clinic? ⌄
You can book online through our website, call us at +91 6361347722, or message us on WhatsApp for a quick response.
What services does Vtiara Clinic offer? ⌄
We offer Hair Transplant, PRP Therapy, Acne Treatment, Pigmentation Treatment, Laser Hair Removal, Anti-Aging Solutions, Skin Rejuvenation, and more.
Who are the dermatologists at Vtiara Clinic? ⌄
Our clinic is led by Dr. Madan Kumar BJ, MD & Co-Founder, a Consultant Dermatologist & Hair Transplant Surgeon with over 15 years of experience.
What happens during my first consultation? ⌄
Your first consultation includes a detailed assessment of your skin or hair concern, a discussion of your medical history, and a personalized treatment plan tailored to your goals.
